# Answer to #10: revision-interval task joins

When overwritten wiki pages are split into coherent revision intervals rather than
evaluated as whole objects, 8 out of 23 cross-task candidate links (34.8%) disappear,
completely eliminating false joins caused by page reuse (including the known
cashier-to-UNCTAD overwrite and five further supported transitions) while preserving
100% of legitimate multi-stage links supported within an interval.

See the [complete source-qualified answer package](10-revision-interval-task-joins/README.md)
for exact before/after record IDs, diff hunks, public record links, quantitative link reduction tables,
and portable offline verification via `verify.py`. Refs #10.
